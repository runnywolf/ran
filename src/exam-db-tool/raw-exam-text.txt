1. Consider the number of nonnegative integer solutions to \(x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=12\), subject to the following constraints:
• \(x_1\) is even;
• \(2 \leq x_2 \leq 4\);
• \(x_3\) is a multiple of 3;
• \(0 \leq x_4 \leq 3\).
How many solutions satisfy these conditions?
(A) 18
(B) 20
(C) 24
(D) 30
(E) 36

2. A binary string of length 10 must satisfy at least one of the following conditions:
• It contains at least four 1’s, or
• It ends with 01.
How many such binary strings exist?
(A) 466
(B) 629
(C) 848
(D) 885
(E) 1104

3. Let \(S\) be a set with 8 distinct elements, and let \(e\) be a fixed element of \(S\).
We consider all subsets of \(S\). How many subsets of \(S\) satisfy at least one of the following two conditions?
• The subset has an odd number of elements;
• The subset contains the element \(e\).
(A) 64
(B) 128
(C) 192
(D) 224
(E) 256

4. Let \(T\) be a full 3‑ary tree, meaning every internal vertex has exactly 3 children. Suppose \(T\) has 82 vertices in total. How many leaf vertices does \(T\) have?
(A) 27
(B) 41
(C) 42
(D) 54
(E) 55

5. Find the unique solution \(x\) modulo 105 that satisfies:

Please determine the value of \(x\) mod 11.
(A) 5
(B) 6
(C) 8
(D) 9
(E) 10

6. Let \(P(x)\) indicates “\(x\) is in the right place”, and \(C(x)\) indicates “\(x\) is in good condition”.
The domain of all quantifiers is the set of your tools. Which of the following translations from logical
expressions into English sentences are false?
(A) \(\exists x(\neg P(x))\): All of my tools are out of place.
(B) \(\exists x(P(x) \land \neg C(x))\): Some tools are in the right place, but not in good condition.
(C) \(\neg \forall x C(x)\): Not all of my tools are in good condition.
(D) \(\forall x(C(x) \to P(x))\): All tools that are out of place are in good condition.
(E) \(\forall x(C(x) \lor P(x))\): Every tool is either in the right place or in good condition.

7. Determine which of the following functions are onto.
• Let \(Z\) be the set of integers \((\ldots,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,\ldots)\).
• Let \(N\) be the set of natural numbers \((0,1,2,3,\ldots)\).
• Let \(Z^+\) be the set of positive integers \((1,2,3,\ldots)\).
• Let \(Q\) be the set of rational numbers.
• Let \(R\) be the set of real numbers.
(A) \(f:Z \to Z\), where \(f(n)=n+1\).
(B) \(f:N \to N\), where \(f(n)=n+1\).
(C) \(f:Z \times Z^+ \to Q\), where \(f(a,b)=a/b\).
(D) \(f:Z \times Z^+ \to R\), where \(f(a,b)=a/b\).
(E) \(f:Q \to R\), where \(f(x)=x\).

8. Let \(\phi(n)\) denote Euler’s totient function, which counts the number of integers between 1 and \(n\)
that are relatively prime to \(n\). Consider the integer \(n=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7\).
Which of the following statements are true?
(A) \(\phi(n)=(2-1)^3\cdot(3-1)^2\cdot(7-1)\).
(B) \(\phi(n)=n\cdot(1-@f{1;2})\cdot(1-@f{1;3})\cdot(1-@f{1;7})\).
(C) \(\phi(n)=2^{3-1}\cdot3^{2-1}\cdot(7-1)\).
(D) \(\phi(n)\) is divisible by 12.
(E) \(\phi(n)=(2-1)^{3-1}\cdot(3-1)^{2-1}\cdot1\).

9. A professor at National Chung Hsing University teaching a Discrete Mathematics course gives a
multiple‑choice quiz with 10 questions, each having 5 choices. Which of the following statements are true?
(A) If two answer sheets are randomly drawn, the probability that the two answer sheets are identical is \(@f{1;5^{10}}\).
(B) If two answer sheets are randomly drawn, the probability that the two answer sheets are different is \(1-@f{1;5^{10}}\).
(C) The maximum number of students the class can have without forcing three identical answer sheets is \(2\cdot6^{10}\).
(D) The minimum number of students needed to guarantee that at least three answer sheets are identical is \(2\cdot5^{10}+1\).
(E) The minimum number of students needed to guarantee that at least four answer sheets are identical is \(3\cdot6^{10}+1\).

10. A binary classifier, the recognizer, is used to identify boys in a group of 15 people, consisting of
9 boys and 6 girls. When the recognizer is tested, it outputs 10 predictions of “boy”, but only 7 of
them are actually boys. All remaining people not predicted as “boy” are predicted as “girl”.
Based on this information, which of the following statements are correct?
(A) The precision of the recognizer is \(@f{7;10}\).
(B) The recall of the recognizer is \(@f{7;9}\).
(C) The false negative rate is \(@f{2;3}\).
(D) The specificity is \(@f{1;2}\).
(E) The F1‑score of the recognizer is \(@f{7;9}\).

11. Let
\[A=@pm{1,2,-1;3,k,4;-2,1,5},\quad\mathbf{b}=@pm{2;-1;3}.\]
Which of the following statements are correct?
(A) Expanding \(\det(A)\) along the first row gives \(\det(A)=1@vm{k,4;1,5}-2@vm{3,4;-2,5}-1@vm{3,k;-2,1}\).
(B) The system \(A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\) has a unique solution whenever \(\det(A)=0\).
(C) Performing a single elementary row operation of the form \(R_2 \leftarrow R_2+cR_1\) does not change the determinant of \(A\), regardless of the value of \(c\).
(D) Cramer’s Rule applies exactly when \(\operatorname{rank}(A)=3\).
(E) If \(\operatorname{rank}(A)=2\), then \(\operatorname{rank}([A|\mathbf{b}])\) must also be 2.

12. Let the following sets be subsets of vector spaces over \(R\). Which of the following sets are linearly independent?
(A) \(\{(1,2,0,1),(3,6,1,3),(0,0,1,0)\}\subset R^4\).
(B) \(\{3,x-2,x^2-2x+1\}\subset P_2\).
(C) \(\left\{@pm{1,3;2,7},@pm{4,2;-1,2},@pm{-1,7;7,19}\right\}\subset M_{2\times2}\).
(D) \(\{\sin x,\cos x,\sin 2x\}\subset C[0,2\pi]\).
(E) \(\{x,|x|,x|x|\}\subset C(R)\).

13. Let \(A,B\in M_n(R)\). Which of the following statements are FALSE?
(A) For any vectors \(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}\in R^n\), the dot product satisfies \(\mathbf{u}^T\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{v}^T\mathbf{u}\).
(B) If \(S\) is skew‑symmetric, then for every vector \(\mathbf{x}\in R^n\), \(\mathbf{x}^TS\mathbf{x}=0\).
(C) If \(A\) and \(B\) are symmetric matrices, then \(AB\) is symmetric.
(D) If \(AB\) is invertible, then both \(A\) and \(B\) are invertible.
(E) If \(A\) is symmetric and invertible, then \(A^{-1}\) is not symmetric.

14. Let \(T:R^3\to R^3\) be a linear transformation whose matrix in the standard basis is
\[A=@pm{4,1,0;0,4,0;0,0,-2}.\]
Which of the following statements are correct?
(A) The kernel of \(T+2I\) has dimension 1.
(B) The range of \(T-4I\) has dimension 1.
(C) The matrix \(A\) is not similar to any diagonal matrix.
(D) Any matrix similar to \(A\) must have only one block for the eigenvalue \(-2\).
(E) The eigenspace associated with eigenvalue 4 is a 2‑dimensional plane in \(R^3\).

15. Let \(V\) be an inner‑product vector space with inner product \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle\).
Which of the following statements are correct?
(A) If \(\|\mathbf{u}\|=0\) for some \(\mathbf{u}\in V\), then \(\mathbf{u}\) does not have to be the zero vector.
(B) For any nonzero vectors \(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}\in V\), the angle \(\theta\) between them satisfies
\(0\leq\theta\leq\pi\) and is uniquely determined by
\(\cos\theta=@f{\langle\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}\rangle;\|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|}\).
(C) An orthonormal basis \(\{e_1,\cdots,e_k\}\) of \(V\) satisfies \(\mathbf{v}=\sum_{i=1}^k\langle\mathbf{v},e_i\rangle e_i\) for every \(\mathbf{v}\in V\).
(D) The projection of a vector onto a subspace can be found by taking inner products with any basis of that subspace.
(E) If two vectors are orthogonal, then their lengths must be equal.

16. Let \(\mathbf{X}\in R^3\) be a zero‑mean random vector representing three correlated sensor channels \((X_1\ X_2\ X_3)^T\). Suppose the covariance matrix of \(\mathbf{X}\) is
\[\Sigma=@pm{3,1,0;1,3,0;0,0,2}.\]
In a signal‑processing pipeline, you want to whiten the data by constructing a linear transformation \(W\) such that the transformed vector
\[\mathbf{Y}=W\mathbf{X}\]
has identity covariance matrix:
\[\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf{Y})=@pm{1,0,0;0,1,0;0,0,1}.\]
(a) Show that \(\Sigma\) is a Hermitian matrix and verify that it also satisfies the properties of a covariance matrix. (2%)
(b) Find the whitening matrix \(W\). (8%)
